**INZOX** is a DeepTech R&D lab, founded in 2018 in Alexandria, Egypt, with branches in the United States and Malaysia. We build intelligent products, automation systems and scalable infrastructure that turn weeks of work into minutes. Our motto is *We Deliver First.*

**What we do**
- **Cloud & FinOps.** Cut cloud spend and tune performance with FinOps best practices.
- **DevOps & Automation.** CI/CD, infrastructure as code and Kubernetes.
- **DevSecOps & Security.** Secure-by-design architecture, compliance and threat protection.
- **AI & Innovation Lab.** Machine learning, NLP, computer vision and intelligent automation.
- **Software Engineering.** Custom software on modern stacks.
- **Tech Academy.** Hands-on training designed by working engineers.

**Products**
- [**ZOX**](https://inzox.ai) is a sovereign enterprise AI brain. It runs inside your own infrastructure: air-gapped, with zero data egress.
- [**OX.Report**](https://ox.report) runs AI technical audits covering security, performance, SEO and UX.
- [**Docker.Boats**](https://docker.boats) is an AI command center for containers.
- [**HOURz**](https://hourz.ai) is a platform where time is the currency.
- [**CVmaker**](https://maker.cv) builds a live CV website in minutes.

**Offices:** Alexandria, Egypt (HQ) · Delaware, USA · Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
